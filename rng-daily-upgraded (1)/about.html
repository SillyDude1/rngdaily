ABOUT · THE NUMBER UNIVERSE

A tiny game
with deep pockets.

RNG//DAILY started with one question: can a random number feel like it has a personality?

ONE AT A TIME

The main experience always centers one number. Generate, look, reveal, react. Multiple rolls are welcome, but the interface never becomes a wall of numbers.

RECOGNITION OVER RUBRICS

A number gets rewarded when a human can explain why it is interesting. 69420 is a cultural reference. 80085 is a calculator joke. 777 is visually loud.

THE ECONOMY

Reveal patterns to earn coins. Spend them on backgrounds or Infinite Mode. Nothing is pay-to-win, and all progress is local to your browser.

RNGDLE INSPIRED

The clean daily-number idea is an original interpretation inspired by the minimal feel of RNGdle.com. This site uses its own code, content, and visual system.
